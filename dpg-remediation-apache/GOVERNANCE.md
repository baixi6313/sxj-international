# Governance (SXJ / 事现鉴)

SXJ is governed as a **neutral public-good protocol**, not a corporation.

## The Ratifier (founder)

- The Ratifier retains **human arbitration** over all binding verification
  verdicts and over changes to the core specification (root→spec).
- This role is deliberately a *steward of neutrality*, not an owner of truth.

## Human-retained ratification layer (MAIP R-1…R-6)

- All binding verification verdicts are made by **humans**. AI may suggest,
  never ratify.
- The six human-retained items (R-1…R-6) cover the irreversible or
  high-consequence decisions in the verification flow.

## Neutrality safeguards

- SXJ dissolves once broadly adopted (root→spec design): it is a catalyst, not
  a permanent institution.
- Donors receive transparency and recognition, **never** control over verdicts
  (see the donation prospectus). Designated public-interest domains accelerate
  verification *capacity*, never the outcome of any single fact.
- The `j` (jurisdiction) axis respects each polity's own verification layer;
  no external hard标尺 is imposed.

## Evolution

- Specification changes follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md); binding
  changes require the Ratifier's arbitration.
- Disagreements are resolved through the human-ratified layer, not automated
  defaults.
