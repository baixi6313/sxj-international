# SXJ (事现鉴) — Potential Foreign / International Donor Institutions

> Research compiled 2026-08-09. All listed organizations are **philanthropic / grant-making — not equity investors**.
> Companion document: `sxj-funding-request-en.html` (the donation prospectus).
> Contact for SXJ: **583272294@qq.com**

SXJ seeks **donations / grants only**. It is a public commons / digital public good rooted in Article 22 of the UDHR (right to social security). Below are the institutions most aligned with its mission, with focus, fit, and how to engage.

---

## 1. Luminate / Omidyar Network
- **Focus:** digital rights, civic technology, holding technology accountable, rebuilding trust in digital platforms. Omidyar Network has committed roughly **US$2 billion** to the societal impact of technology.
- **Fit:** SXJ's neutral verification layer directly addresses "trust in the digital public square."
- **Engage:** Omidyar Network (`omidyarnetwork.org`) and Luminate (`luminategroup.com`) accept inquiries; much grantmaking is relationship / invitation-based. Luminate funds digital-rights organizations.

## 2. Filecoin Foundation / Protocol Labs (ProPGF & RetroPGF)
- **Focus:** public-goods funding for decentralized infrastructure; quadratic-funding rounds; developer grants up to ~US$50k; ProPGF batches ~US$3.6M across the ecosystem; RetroPGF rewards proven impact.
- **Fit:** SXJ's decentralized verification protocol is a public good; RetroPGF rewards demonstrated impact.
- **Engage:** `filecoin.io` / `protocol.ai` grants; Gitcoin-style quadratic-funding rounds; apply with a public-goods proposal.

## 3. Digital Public Goods Alliance (DPGA)
- **Focus:** UN-endorsed multi-stakeholder alliance to fund, discover, and promote Digital Public Goods (DPGs); secretariat funded by the Gates Foundation and Norad.
- **Fit:** SXJ qualifies as a digital public good (open source, open data, do-no-harm). Listing in the DPG Registry opens financing and government-adoption pathways.
- **Engage:** `digitalpublicgoods.net` — nominate SXJ as a DPG; contact the secretariat at `hello@digitalpublicgoods.net`.

## 4. Craig Newmark Philanthropies
- **Focus:** trustworthy journalism, fighting disinformation, cybersecurity, voter protection. Has given **US$100M+** since 2016 (Craigslist founder); Cyber Civil Defense initiative.
- **Fit:** SXJ's anti-disinformation / verifiable-facts mission.
- **Engage:** `craignewmarkphilanthropies.org` — grants often via invited proposals; align with the "trustworthy information" portfolio.

## 5. MacArthur Foundation
- **Focus:** Technology in the Public Interest; journalism, democracy, AI policy. Grants typically **US$200K–$2M**; strong field-building grants.
- **Fit:** SXJ as public-interest technology / verification infrastructure.
- **Engage:** `macfound.org` — LOI via program staff; largely invitation-based.

## 6. Open Society Foundations
- **Focus:** democracy, human rights, fighting online misinformation (~US$4M grants); co-committed **US$200M** (with Ford, Hewlett, MacArthur, Packard) for public-interest AI.
- **Fit:** UDHR-rooted mission; anti-disinformation.
- **Engage:** `opensocietyfoundations.org` — program officers in democracy & digital programs.

## 7. Ford Foundation
- **Focus:** public-interest technology, tech accountability, inequality. Grants **US$100K–$1M+**.
- **Fit:** SXJ as public-interest tech for the global commons.
- **Engage:** `fordfoundation.org` — relationship-based.

## 8. Knight Foundation
- **Focus:** informed communities, journalism, technology & democracy. **Multiple open RFPs per year**; grants US$50K–$500K.
- **Fit:** trustworthy information ecosystem.
- **Engage:** `knightfoundation.org` — watch active RFPs.

## 9. Patrick J. McGovern Foundation
- **Focus:** AI & data science for social good, responsible AI, data stewardship. Grants **US$100K–$750K**; global scope.
- **Fit:** SXJ's AI-assisted, human-ratified verification; data stewardship.
- **Engage:** `mcgovern.org` — open calls + targeted RFPs; demonstrate responsible-AI capacity.

## 10. UNICEF Innovation Fund / UNDP
- **Focus:** **equity-free seed funding** for startups in emerging markets building DPGs; digital public goods.
- **Fit:** SXJ as a DPG, especially for deployment in low- and middle-income contexts.
- **Engage:** `unicef.org/innovation/venturefund` — apply with an open-source DPG proposal.

## 11. Berggruen Institute
- **Focus:** philosophy, future of humanity, planetary governance; fellowships & prizes (not typical project grants).
- **Fit:** the *ideas* layer — UDHR Article 22, social security as a human right, global governance.
- **Engage:** `berggruen.org` — fellowship / essay programmes; suitable for thought-leadership, not infrastructure funding.

## 12. Mozilla Foundation
- **Focus:** internet health, open source, trustworthy AI, misinformation.
- **Fit:** open verification protocol as internet infrastructure.
- **Engage:** `mozilla.org` — MOSS (Mozilla Open Source Support) & advocacy grants.
