1. Executive Summary

SXJ (事现鉴, "the witnessing of events") is an open, neutral protocol that lets any person or institution record and verify public facts — events that affect the common good — using a shared coordinate system (the "light-cone coordinates": type × tier × jurisdiction × organization + time + verifiable density ρ). It is built to support the realisation of Article 22 of the UDHR — the right to social security and to the economic, social and cultural rights indispensable for dignity.

We are not seeking investment or equity. SXJ is a public commons / digital public good. We seek philanthropic donations — unrestricted or earmarked — to build and sustain the verification infrastructure that lets societies trust shared facts without depending on any single platform, government, or corporation.

A distinguishing feature of our model: donors may designate a category of public-interest projects to be prioritized and featured on SXJ (see §5). This lets a funder accelerate verification capacity in the domain they care about — environmental protection, anti-disinformation, or labour & social security — while SXJ's neutrality and human-ratification layer remain strictly intact.

2. What SXJ Is

  • A protocol, not a platform owner. SXJ defines how to verify, not who decides truth. Anyone can implement it.
  • Light-cone coordinate model. Every verified public event is a point (x, y, z, t, ρ, j): three-value type (natural / humanistic / digital), three tiers (system / professional / daily), jurisdiction j (incl. China, Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan as parts of China; and foreign jurisdictions), verifying organization, time, and verifiable density ρ.
  • Three values. 共济值 (solidarity value, survival right, need-based) · 贡献值 (contribution value, work-based, net positive) · 负贡献 (negative contribution, accountability).
  • MAIP verification protocol with a human-retained ratification layer (R-1…R-6): AI may suggest, humans ratify.
  • A digital public good. Open specification, open data, no proprietary lock-in.

3. Why a Donation, Not Equity

  • SXJ is global public infrastructure in the spirit of the UN Digital Public Goods definition. It is not a venture to be owned or traded.
  • We will not issue shares, equity-like tokens, or profit-sharing. Funding is received as donations / grants.
  • Governance stays with the project's Ratifier (founder, retained human arbitration) and the human-retained layers of MAIP. Donors receive transparency and recognition, not control over verdicts.
  • This protects the core promise: a verifiable public-fact layer that no single interest can capture.

4. Use of Funds

  1. Verification infrastructure — the open MAIP tooling, validator SDKs, and multilingual support.
  2. Validator network — onboarding civil-society, academic, and professional organizations as verifying nodes across jurisdictions.
  3. Mobile & web apps — keeping the app and mini-program free and open.
  4. Designated-domain acceleration — tooling + localization for donor-prioritized public-interest domains (§5).
  5. Open research — the coordinate model, anti-disinformation methodology, and social-security metrics.
  6. Operations & transparency — the public ledger, audits, and the Light-Cone Operations Office.

5. Donor Benefit — Designated Priority Domain (指定某类项目优先呈现)

Donors may earmark their gift to accelerate verification for a specific category of public-interest projects, which is then featured / prioritized on SXJ as a Sponsored Public-Interest Collection.

How it works

  • The donation funds verification capacity (validators, tooling, localization, outreach) for that domain — not the verdict of any single fact.
  • The domain receives a featured collection on SXJ and in the app, visibly credited to the donor.
  • Neutrality safeguards: (a) designation selects which public-interest domains get resources, never the outcome of any verification; (b) all entries still pass the same MAIP human-ratified verification; (c) the human-retained layers (R-1…R-6) are unchanged; (d) the collection is labelled "donor-designated" for full transparency.

Example domains donors have found resonant

  • Environmental protection & ecological contribution (aligned with China's 2021 ecological-product value mechanism)
  • Anti-disinformation / trustworthy public information
  • Labour rights & social-security verification
  • Open knowledge & civic education

Donation amount — fully at the donor's discretion (金额自定义)

A gift may be one-time or recurring, unrestricted or earmarked to a designated public-interest domain (see above). The amount is custom: the donor states the figure; we do not impose a threshold.
