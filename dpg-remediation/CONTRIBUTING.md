# Contributing to SXJ (事现鉴)

Thank you for your interest in strengthening a public-good verification protocol.

## Principles

- SXJ is a **neutral catalyst**, not an owner. Contributions should advance the
  shared specification and tooling, never capture the verdict of any fact.
- All binding verification verdicts are **human-ratified** (MAIP R-1…R-6).
  Code or content that automates a binding verdict will not be accepted.

## How to contribute

1. **Open an issue** describing the proposed change (spec, tooling, docs, or
   localization).
2. **Discuss** with maintainers; for spec changes, the Ratifier's arbitration
   is required.
3. **Fork & PR** against the `main` branch of the relevant repository.
4. Ensure your contribution is **licensed under MIT** (see LICENSE) and that you
   have the right to contribute it.

## Scope

- Welcome: reference implementations, validator SDKs, multilingual support,
  documentation, research, and designated public-interest domain tooling.
- Out of scope: anything that would let a single interest control verification
  outcomes, or that introduces equity / token-as-equity mechanics.
