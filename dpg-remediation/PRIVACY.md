# Privacy Policy (SXJ / 事现鉴)

SXJ is a verification protocol. This statement describes its privacy posture.

## Data minimisation by design

- Verification payloads are **public facts + verifiable density ρ**, not
  personal profiles.
- The protocol is designed so that **no personally identifying information
  (PII)** is required to record or verify a public event.
- Where a person is the *subject* of a public event, the event records the
  fact and its verification trail — not the person's identity beyond what is
  already publicly attributable.

## Lawful basis & applicable law

- SXJ aims to align with applicable privacy law, including the EU GDPR where
  relevant, and local data-protection law in each jurisdiction `j`.
- No data is sold. SXJ receives funding only as donations/grants (see NOTICE).

## Your rights

- Individuals may request review of how they are represented in a verified
  public event through the human-ratification layer (MAIP R-1…R-6).
- Incorrect or unverifiable entries can be challenged and, where warranted,
  corrected or removed via the same human layer.
